RULES FOR THE IMPLEMENTING SESSION
Use only #000000 and #FFFFFF for the brand mark and wordmark.
Keep graph colors for semantic states, agents, tracks or status; do not tint the logo.
Choose black or white logo according to background contrast.
Do not stretch, rotate, add shadows, gradients, outlines or effects.
Keep clear space of at least 1/4 of the symbol height around the lockup.
Minimum recommended symbol size: 16 px; horizontal logo: 128 px wide.
Below 24 px prefer the standalone symbol. Validate in the actual UI.
SVG logos and symbols are all paths, including lettering; no font installation needed.
Wordmark base: Helvetica Neue Medium, optically spaced and converted to paths.
The font software is not included. Presentation-board labels are live text.
Tagline (optional, separate from logo): Explore freely. Ship with proof.
No changes have been made to the product, repository, or protected Claude workspace.
