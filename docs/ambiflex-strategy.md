# Ambiflex: adoption strategy

Recorded in the plugin's tree (nodes n9 to n15). Horizon: 90 days. Run it with `/ambiflex auto` once reviewed; the plugin reports the end-to-end ETA when it starts.

## The goal behind the buzz (Altitude)

"The most used plugin" is not the real goal. The real goal: **be the person who showed, with data, how a human keeps control of agents**. The plugin is the proof of that idea. The people Anthropic notices are not those with the most stars, but those who made public an idea the team wishes it had built.

So every action below serves **the idea** first (keeping control of agents, estimating better with each project), and the tool second.

## Honesty about "Anthropic calls me"

Nobody can guarantee it, and aiming at the hire directly makes the content worse. What is controllable: producing public, measured, useful work on the exact subject the Claude Code team is exploring (Felix Rieseberg published his tutorial on 13 October 2026; the plugin API is in early access, so the team is looking for feedback). Being the best field report on those two subjects is within reach.

## What a launch expert would do (Depth)

| What amateurs do | What people who get noticed do |
|---|---|
| Announce the tool the day it compiles | Announce when they have **measured proof** |
| "Look at my plugin" | "Here is what I learned running 3 agents in parallel, with data" |
| One post, then silence | A **series**: one lesson a week, the plugin in the background |
| Count stars | Count **issues opened by others** and external PRs |

## The five workstreams

### n10: proof before promotion (2 weeks)
Before any launch, run **a real project** with the plugin: 3 tracks explored in parallel by agents, an external judge per track, estimated vs. actual shown. Output: a 60-second video of the mobile page, no marketing voice-over. This is the centrepiece of everything else. Depends on Phases 2 and 3 of the technical plan (`ambiflex-plan.md`): without history and calibration there is nothing new to show.

### n11: content is worth more than the plugin (4 weeks, in parallel)
A series of 4 short articles, one a week, on your site and relayed on X / LinkedIn:
1. "Why 'done' means nothing when an agent says it" (the `review` status and the external judge).
2. "My estimates ran ×1.6 optimistic: what 20 nodes taught me" (real calibration data).
3. "Running 3 agents like a chess engine" (parallel exploration, pruning).
4. "What I learned about Claude Code's plugin API" (field feedback useful to the team: bugs, gaps, what works). **The article most likely to be read inside Anthropic.**

Each article carries a verifiable number and a screenshot of the panel.

### n12: distribution (2 weeks, around the launch)
In order of expected impact:
1. **Reply directly to Felix's tutorial** on X, with the video: "I took your method literally and turned it into a panel inside Claude Code". The most precise target: the person who wrote the method and leads engineering of the desktop app where the panel shows.
2. Submission to the official Claude Code plugin directory (prepared in Phase 6).
3. The `awesome-claude-code` lists and equivalents (one PR each).
4. Show HN, a Tuesday or Wednesday morning US time, factual title: "Show HN: A reasoning-tree pane for Claude Code that keeps humans in control of agents".
5. Claude Code Discord and forums, r/ClaudeAI: one post, no spam.
6. A French-language write-up (francophone AI communities): less competition, a different angle.

### n13: the retention loop (3 weeks; this is the technical plan)
Buzz brings installs; **usage** comes from the plugin getting more useful with every project: history (Phase 2) and estimate calibration (Phase 3). Without them it is a pretty graph people uninstall after a week. Top priority before launch.

### n14: measure (external judge, continuous)
Measurable 90-day targets:
- 500 stars on the dedicated repository.
- 10 external contributors (at least one merged PR each).
- 20 issues opened by people other than you (a sign of real use).
- 1 mention by someone at Anthropic (retweet, reply, issue).
If at 45 days no indicator is halfway, change the angle (no new feature: new content).

### n15: launch day (1 day)
Everything ready beforehand. Same morning: video, article 1, public repository with an English README, the reply to Felix, Show HN. Then answer every comment within 24 h, and record each piece of feedback in the tree as a node.
